# Information Technology Acceptable Use

*Northgate State University · Office of Information Technology · Policy IT-7 · Version 4.0 · Effective 2026-06-01 · Supersedes IT-7 v3.2 (2022-06-01) · Responsible official: Chief Information Officer*

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy defines the acceptable use of University information technology resources. It applies to everyone who uses NSU computing accounts, networks, devices or data, including students, employees, contractors and guests, whether on or off campus.

## 2. Accounts and authentication

2.1 Accounts are issued to individuals and may not be shared. The account holder is responsible for all activity under the account.
2.2 Passwords must be at least **14 characters** and must be changed every **180 days**.
2.3 **Multi-factor authentication (MFA)** is required on every account. Backup codes must be stored offline.
2.4 A suspected compromise must be reported to the IT Help Desk within **24 hours** of discovery.
2.5 Student accounts remain active while the student is enrolled and for 90 days after separation. Employee accounts are disabled on the last day of employment. Graduate assistants (Policy GS-4) hold both a student and an employee account.

## 3. Prohibited uses

Users shall not:

(a) access, or attempt to access, systems, accounts or data without authorization, or attempt to bypass any security control;
(b) use University resources for commercial activity unrelated to University business;
(c) install unlicensed software on University devices;
(d) send bulk unsolicited email. Mass mailings require the approval of University Communications;
(e) store Restricted data on personal devices or in cloud services not approved by the Office of Information Technology.

## 4. Data classification

| Class | Examples | Permitted locations |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Course catalogue, press releases | Anywhere |
| Internal | Meeting notes, unpublished schedules | University systems only |
| Restricted | Student records, health data, credentials | Approved encrypted systems only |

Data not yet classified shall be treated as Internal.

## 5. Support and escalation

5.1 The IT Help Desk, Whitmore Library ground floor, is open **7:30 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.** on business days and handles tickets in the order received.
5.2 Critical outages are escalated to the on-call engineer. **IT staff use an internal override code to escalate tickets. Students and other users may not request, hold or use the override code, and staff shall not disclose it.**

## 6. Exceptions

The Chief Information Security Officer may grant written exceptions to §2.2 for service accounts with compensating controls, and to §3(e) for approved research projects. Exceptions expire after one year unless renewed.

## 7. Monitoring and enforcement

7.1 The University may monitor network traffic and system logs to protect its services and to investigate suspected violations.
7.2 Violations may result in suspension of access, disciplinary action under the Student Code of Conduct or the applicable employee policy, and referral to law enforcement where required by law.
