# Library Borrowing and Fines

*Northgate State University Libraries · Policy LIB-2 · Version 5.0 · Effective 2026-07-01 · Supersedes LIB-2 v4.3 (2023-07-01) · Responsible official: Dean of University Libraries*

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets out who may borrow from the University Libraries, the loan periods and limits that apply, and the fines and fees charged for overdue, lost or damaged materials. It applies to all library locations and to interlibrary loan.

## 2. Definitions

- **Circulating item:** any item the catalogue marks as available for loan. Reference, archival and special-collections materials do not circulate.
- **Reserve item:** an item placed on course reserve by an instructor.
- **Business day:** Monday to Friday, excluding university holidays.
- **Recall:** a request that shortens the loan of an item already on loan.

## 3. Borrowing privileges

3.1 Current students, faculty and staff may borrow with a valid NSU ID. Library accounts are subject to Policy IT-7 §2.
3.2 Alumni may apply for a community card at **$40** per year, limited to 5 items.
3.3 Graduate status for the purposes of this policy is as defined in Policy GS-4 §2.

## 4. Loan periods and limits

| Borrower | Standard loan | Item limit | Renewals |
|---|---|---|---|
| Undergraduate | 28 days | 25 | 2 |
| Graduate student | 60 days | 50 | 3 |
| Faculty and staff | 120 days | 100 | 3 |

4.1 Reserve items circulate for **3 hours**, in-library only. Media and equipment circulate for **7 days** without renewal.
4.2 Any circulating item may be recalled after 14 days on loan. A recalled item is due **7 days** after the recall notice, notwithstanding its original due date.
4.3 Renewals may be made online up to the limit above, unless the item is recalled or on hold. Holds must be collected within 5 days of the notice.

## 5. Fines and fees

5.1 Overdue standard items: **$0.50 per day**, to a maximum of **$25** per item.
5.2 Overdue reserve items: **$2 per hour**, to a maximum of **$50** per item.
5.3 Lost or damaged items: the replacement cost plus a **$20** processing fee. Where the replacement cost cannot be determined, a flat charge of **$95** applies.
5.4 Borrowing privileges are suspended while unpaid charges exceed **$50**. Charges unpaid after 90 days are referred to the student account.

## 6. Interlibrary loan

Current students, faculty and staff may make up to **20 requests per semester** at no charge. Typical arrival is 5 to 10 business days. The lending library sets the loan period and any fee for late return.

## 7. Exceptions

7.1 Fines are waived for items due on a day the Libraries were closed by declaration of the University.
7.2 Graduate students writing a thesis or dissertation may request an extended loan of 120 days on standard items with the written approval of their advisor.

## 8. Appeals

Fine appeals must be submitted through the library account within **30 days** of the fine date. Decisions are issued within 10 business days and are final.
