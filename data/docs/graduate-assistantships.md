# Graduate Assistantships and Tuition Waivers

*Northgate State University · Office of Graduate Studies · Policy GS-4 · Version 3.2 · Effective 2026-08-01 · Supersedes GS-4 v3.1 (2024-08-01) · Responsible official: Dean of Graduate Studies*

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy governs the appointment, compensation and tuition benefits of graduate assistants at Northgate State University ("NSU" or "the University"). It applies to all academic and administrative units that appoint graduate assistants and to all students holding such appointments.

## 2. Definitions

- **Graduate assistant (GA):** a full-time, degree-seeking graduate student appointed to teach, conduct research or provide professional services under the supervision of a faculty or staff member.
- **FTE:** full-time equivalent. A 0.50 FTE appointment corresponds to 20 hours of service per week; 0.25 FTE to 10 hours.
- **Academic year:** the fall and spring semesters taken together. Summer is a separate term.
- **Full-time enrollment:** 9 credit hours in fall or spring; 6 in summer.

## 3. Appointments

3.1 Appointments shall be half-time (0.50 FTE) or quarter-time (0.25 FTE). No GA may hold appointments totalling more than 0.50 FTE during fall or spring.
3.2 Each appointment shall be documented in an appointment letter stating the FTE, the dates, the stipend and any tuition waivers awarded.
3.3 Units shall submit fall appointments by **August 1** and spring appointments by **December 1**.

## 4. Eligibility

4.1 A GA must maintain full-time enrollment and a cumulative GPA of **3.0** or higher.
4.2 International students must hold valid F-1 or J-1 status. Total on-campus employment, including the assistantship, may not exceed 20 hours per week while classes are in session. Account eligibility for employee systems is governed by Policy IT-7 §2.

## 5. Stipends

5.1 The minimum stipend for a half-time appointment is **$18,500** per academic year; for a quarter-time appointment, **$9,250**. Units may pay above the minimum.
5.2 Stipends are paid biweekly. Summer appointments are paid separately at the same hourly rate.

## 6. Tuition waivers

6.1 **Matriculation waiver.** Covers base tuition. Awarded automatically to every GA appointed at 0.25 FTE or above.
6.2 **Out-of-state waiver.** Covers the non-resident tuition differential. Awarded as funding permits. A waiver not stated in the appointment letter has not been awarded.
6.3 Waivers do not cover student fees (**$1,120** per semester at 9 credits), health insurance or course-specific charges.
6.4 A waiver is cancelled for the semester if the appointment ends, or enrollment drops below full-time, before the **fourth week of classes**. After that date the waiver stands for the semester.
6.5 Waiver forms must reach the Office of Graduate Studies by the **fifth day of classes**.

## 7. Exceptions

Exceptions to §3.1 and §4.1 require the written approval of the Dean of Graduate Studies. No exception to the 20-hour limit in §4.2 is available to international students.
