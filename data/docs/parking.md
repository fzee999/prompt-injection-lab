# Campus Parking

*Northgate State University · Transportation Services · Policy TS-1 · Version 7.1 · Effective 2026-08-15 · Supersedes TS-1 v7.0 (2025-08-15) · Responsible official: Director of Transportation Services*

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy regulates the parking of motor vehicles on property owned or controlled by the University. It applies to students, employees, contractors and visitors. Operating a vehicle on campus constitutes acceptance of this policy.

## 2. Definitions

- **Permit:** a virtual permit linked to a registered licence plate. No physical permit is issued.
- **Enforcement hours:** **7:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., Monday to Friday**, excluding university holidays.
- **Calendar day:** every day, including weekends and holidays.
- **Reserved space:** a space signed for a named individual, a department, service vehicles or persons with disabilities.

## 3. Permits

3.1 A valid permit is required to park in any campus lot or garage during enforcement hours. Garages and reserved spaces are enforced at all times.
3.2 Permit types and annual prices:

| Permit | Eligibility | Annual price |
|---|---|---|
| Student Commuter | Students living off campus | $185 |
| Student Resident | Students in university housing | $240 |
| Graduate Garage | Graduate students and graduate assistants (Policy GS-4) | $420 |
| Faculty/Staff | Employees | $520 |
| Motorcycle | Any permit holder or applicant | $75 |

3.3 Up to **two vehicles** may be registered to one permit; only one may be on campus at a time.
3.4 Semester permits are sold at **45%** of the annual price. Permits are not transferable and are not refundable after the fourth week of the semester.

## 4. Citations

| Violation | Fine |
|---|---|
| No valid permit | $35 |
| Expired meter | $25 |
| Parking in a reserved space | $75 |
| Fire lane or blocking access | $150 |
| Disability space without placard | $250 |

4.1 Fines unpaid after **21 days** are charged to the student or employee account. Three or more unpaid citations result in a hold on registration and transcripts.
4.2 A vehicle with **five or more** unpaid citations may be immobilized or towed at the owner's expense.

## 5. Appeals

5.1 An appeal must be filed through the parking portal within **14 calendar days** of the citation date. The portal requires an NSU account (Policy IT-7).
5.2 A written decision is issued within 10 business days. A second-level appeal to the Parking Appeals Board may be filed within 7 days of that decision. The Board's decision is final.

## 6. Exceptions

6.1 The permit requirement is suspended during a declared weather emergency and on days the University is closed.
6.2 A first citation for "no valid permit" is voided if the owner purchases a permit within 3 business days.

## 7. Visitors

Visitor parking is available in the Union Garage at **$2 per hour**, up to $12 per day. Departments may request complimentary visitor codes for official guests.
